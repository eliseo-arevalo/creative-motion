# Licences of third-party assets

This repo ships **no third-party audio, fonts files, logos or images**. Fonts come from `@remotion/google-fonts` (open licences), audio is synthesised by `templates/scripts/audio/synth.mjs`, voices are generated by the user's own provider account at setup.

If you add third-party material to a video (music, SFX, an official store badge), keep evidence here, in a per-asset note: source URL, asset id, licence name and link, date accessed, sha256 of the file, the video it was used in. Do not commit the asset itself unless its licence explicitly allows redistribution in a public repository (stock-music sites generally do not). Store badges (e.g. Google Play, App Store) must be used unmodified and as the vendor's brand guidelines require; they are not committed.

See `docs/AUDIO.md` and `packages/motion-kit/src/audio.md`.
